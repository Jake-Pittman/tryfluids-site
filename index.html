Health signal

What PhenoAge means

PhenoAge is a bloodwork-based estimate of how your body is aging compared with your calendar age. It combines common lab markers into one plain-language signal for overall health load. Fluids uses it as one input, not a diagnosis, to help guide nutrition priorities.
